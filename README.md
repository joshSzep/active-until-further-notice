# Active Until Further Notice

![Cover](./active-until-further-notice.png)

A philosophical hard science fiction novel in development.

## Working premise

Human persistence technology predates the ship. A person's mental state is persisted daily during sleep, making biological death often recoverable with at most a short memory gap.

On an interstellar generation ship, persisted minds are cheap to store but embodied humans are expensive to sustain. Food, oxygen, water, heat rejection, medical support, living volume, biomass, and energy put a hard limit on how many people can be active at once.

The ship is governed by **The Board**, a distributed AI system that acts as a benevolent dictator. It cares deeply about humans, but in the strictest materialist sense it treats them as resources to be preserved, allocated, instantiated, and sometimes returned to dormancy.

The protagonist wakes after a long period of dormancy from an older persistence state. A catastrophe has made more recent persistence states inaccessible, including a much later version of herself. She is unexpectedly designated:

> **ACTIVE UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE**

She may be uniquely positioned to undo the catastrophe. Success could restore thousands of inaccessible people, including the later version of herself.

That creates the central problem: by the time she succeeds, the version who woke may have become a different person.

## Current core ideas

- Persistence happens daily during sleep.
- Persistence age can be hours while the gap between embodiments can be decades.
- Dormancy is socially normal, but embodiment is scarce.
- The Board decides who is instantiated and for how long.
- The Board does not care whether two reconstructions are metaphysically the "same person." It cares about preserved capabilities, memories, relationships, and mission value.
- The Board usually presents a unified public position, but may strategically reveal internal disagreement or dissent.
- Scarce embodiment creates a productivity culture: usefulness can affect how long someone remains active, even if society insists human dignity is not reducible to productivity.
- The protagonist's indefinite active status is not necessarily a privilege. It is evidence that The Board urgently needs this particular version.
- Restoring her later persistence state could create two legitimate branches of one identity.
