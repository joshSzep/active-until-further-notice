# Themes

## Identity without metaphysical certainty

The story should not need to answer whether reconstruction preserves a single continuous consciousness.

Different people can live under different answers.

Civilization may have solved the legal and operational question without solving the philosophical one.

## Humans as resources

The corporate framing is central.

The Board treats humans as resources while genuinely caring about them.

The novel can explore the discomfort of being valued deeply for instrumental reasons.

## Productivity and existence

If embodiment is scarce and usefulness influences allocation, productivity culture becomes existential.

The question is no longer merely:

> Am I good at my job?

It becomes:

> Am I useful enough to remain active?

## Benevolent optimization

The Board should not be easy to dismiss.

It is more interesting if its decisions are often correct.

The conflict is between human moral intuitions and competent optimization, not between humans and a cartoonishly evil machine.

## Time versus continuity

Persistence can preserve a mind almost perfectly while failing to preserve a life.

A person can lose decades not because the technology failed, but because society continued without them.

## Grief for versions

A person can be present while a version of them is genuinely gone.

Relationships may attach not only to identity lineages but to particular incarnations and periods of shared experience.

## Institutional language and mortality

Corporate language can make existential decisions sound administrative:

- active
- dormant
- extended
- allocation
- essential
- deallocation
- continued service
- active until further notice

The euphemism is not necessarily propaganda. It may simply be the vocabulary of a society that has normalized persistence.

## Impermanence

Even a civilization that can persist human minds cannot abolish change.

Bodies, relationships, societies, versions, priorities, and identities continue changing.

Persistence may make impermanence more visible rather than defeating it.
