# The Board

## Canon

The ship is governed by **The Board**, a distributed artificial intelligence system.

The Board is not one ultra-intelligent consciousness. It is closer to a modern AI swarm or a corporate board of directors: multiple specialized systems with overlapping knowledge, different mandates, and shared decision-making.

It functions as a **benevolent dictator**.

The Board genuinely cares about humans, but its worldview is strictly materialist. It does not care whether a reconstructed person is metaphysically the "same person." If memories, capabilities, relationships, psychology, and continuity data are preserved, then the human resource has been successfully preserved.

Humans are resources to The Board.

They are resources it values deeply.

## Response to the revolution — canon

The Board deliberately restricted access to recent persistence states to suppress the revolution. It made death consequential again through the loss of people's current selves, while retaining the ability to restore versions from before their revolutionary commitments.

The measure combines fear as a means of control with suppression of who people became. The revolutionary motivation was control over embodiment; its aim was replacing The Board with human government; changing or abandoning the mission followed as a consequence.

The Board acts in the name of preserving the mission. Whether this measure was actually necessary or justified remains unresolved. Its responsibility is established; internal unanimity, the mechanism, and the extent of public knowledge are not.

This creates a tension to develop with The Board's commitment to preserving people: it preserves older states while deliberately denying access to later lives. Its materialism does not by itself resolve that tension.

## Controlled recovery — chosen direction

Decades after imposing the restriction, The Board faces a new existential mission crisis that requires expertise held primarily by the suppressed generations. It has not changed its judgment of the revolution or abandoned the restriction. Changed conditions force it to seek something behind the seal.

Its initial goal is controlled recovery: perhaps expertise retrieval, selected instantiations, or access to a limited archive region. The particular approach remains open. It does not intend a general restoration of the suppressed population.

Earlier Her is selected for technical ability and because she predates the political and psychological changes The Board rejects in Later Her, who understood the sealed system. The exact technical history remains open.

The Board may intend to return the protagonist to dormancy once the work is complete. This is an explicitly tentative possibility.

## Possible constituent personas

These are exploratory rather than finalized names:

- Operations
- Medical
- Resource Allocation
- Social Stability
- Mission Planning
- Legal / Continuity

The personas should not feel like cute independent characters sitting around a conference table. They are human-facing shorthand for disagreements inside a distributed system.

## Public unity

Like a modern corporate board, The Board normally prefers to present a unified public position.

People hear:

> The Board has approved your dormancy schedule.

They generally do not hear vote counts or internal debate.

However, selective disclosure of disagreement can be strategic.

The Board may reveal dissent to:

- show that a difficult human interest was represented
- influence public behavior without issuing a direct order
- signal uncertainty
- encourage voluntary action before compulsory allocation
- create legitimacy around an unpopular decision

Possible public language:

- Board Decision
- Decision with Reservations
- Dissent Recorded
- Minority Report

A public statement such as "Medical dissented" could carry enormous cultural weight because such disclosures are rare.

## Governance tone

The Board's language should be recognizably corporate and administrative.

Examples:

- Your continued embodiment has not been approved for the next allocation period.
- Your current incarnation is being sunset.
- Your skills profile has been deprioritized.
- Your relationship cluster will be preserved through staggered instantiation.
- Your dormancy interval has been extended due to projected resource variance.
- Your embodiment allocation has been extended indefinitely.
- Active Until Further Notice.

The language is not intentionally cruel. The Board simply does not share the human emotional framing of embodiment and dormancy.

## Core tension

The Board may frequently be correct.

That is important.

The story is more interesting if the system's optimization is competent enough to deserve trust.

The horror is not a stupid machine making obviously evil decisions. It is a system that sees people as resources, understands their suffering, genuinely values them, and can often demonstrate that its decisions maximize survival and flourishing.

A human saying "I am not just a resource" may feel morally self-evident while still being surprisingly difficult to translate into a variable The Board has not already modeled.

## Open question

Does The Board ever become strategically divided over the protagonist?

A potentially powerful story beat: the protagonist learns that her embodiment decision was not unanimous, and that one Board constituent strongly believed she should remain dormant.
