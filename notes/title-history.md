# Title History

## Continuity Error

The first working title.

It came from the identity problem around reconstruction but eventually felt too much like the name of a software bug and too narrow for the society the story was becoming.

## Persistence

A strong intermediate title.

It worked on several levels:

- persistent computational state
- biological survival
- psychological endurance
- preservation of identity
- the ship's mission to preserve humans

It remains a useful name for the core technology/concept.

## The Version That Wakes

A later working title.

It captured the philosophical question around reconstruction:

Which version wakes, and why should anyone assume that waking version is identical to the person who slept?

It became too narrow once the story grew toward embodiment scarcity, governance, labor, and The Board.

## Active Until Further Notice

Current working title.

The phrase sounds like ordinary administrative language while referring to continued embodied existence.

It implies:

- embodiment is a status
- embodiment is conditional
- someone else controls when the condition ends
- the protagonist's indefinite active status is unusual
- continued life can be framed as a resource-allocation decision

It also naturally sounds like something The Board would say.
